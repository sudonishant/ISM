The Decks & Landmarks, In Detail

Explore every key location marked on the official Ravenspire chart.

The Chart Vault

Houses 12,000 maritime charts, secret naval routes, and stellar globes high in the red bluffs.

Crow’s Nest

360-degree lookout tower among palm canopies housing 37,000 nautical tomes and avian roosts.

The Krakenarium

Isolated research atoll where marine biologists study kraken tentacles, leviathans, and sirens.

The Harbor

The flagship basin holding 1,200 ships, trade markets, assemblies, and the weekly bounty count.

The Quarterdeck

Senior command post and heavy artillery proving grounds overlooking the Western Approaches.

The Drydock

Shipwrights, hull scrapers, and sailmakers restoring wooden galleons in the protected coral lock.

Crew Quarters

1,200 swinging hammocks, sea chests, dueling rings, and deckhand quarters. Sleep with one eye open.

The Galley

Salt beef, roasted boar, spiced citrus, and non-alcoholic grog served beneath the black flag.

The Treasure Vault

Heavily fortified sea cave guarding 37,000 doubloons and priceless relics behind iron portcullises.

X Marks The Spot

The founder's secret cache etched in blood-red ink. Only accessible under auspicious tides.

The Bounty Chest

Spanish escudos and jewels available to any cadet who solves the collegiate navigational riddle.

Siren’s Boneyard

Bleached rib cages and skull warning totems reminding captains of the unforgiving shoals.
