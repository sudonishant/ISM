The Mentorship Law

Every Legend mentors two first-timers per year. It is the law. It has never once been skipped, and it has been skipped exactly once, by a legend who was at sea, which counts as excused.

Mentorship takes the form of a long walk, a shorter lecture, and a demonstration of the one knot the first-timer keeps getting wrong. The demonstration is always in slow motion. The knot is always a figure-eight.

The parrot is the exception. He does not mentor. He grades.

— The Legends’ Table, by lantern
"They taught me the knot. The knot taught me the rest. Now I teach two first-timers the knot. It goes around forever, like the sea."— A Legend, Class of 1742
"Applied to the mentorship program. My mentor is a lighthouse. He says little. He says nothing. I have never been better led."— A first-timer, Class of 2026
